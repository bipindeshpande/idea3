- Founder story uses "I" but team/company info is missing
- Very brief - only ~300 words of actual content
- No concrete details about the product/technology
- Missing key About page elements (team, timeline, metrics)

**Missing Sections:**
- How the AI/system actually works (brief technical overview)
- Success stories or user testimonials
- Company/timeline information
- Team information (even if just founder)
- Contact/connect information
- Trust signals (users, ideas validated, etc.)

### 2. **Engagement Elements**

**Missing:**

- Links to related pages (Product, Pricing, Contact)
- Visual elements beyond icons
- Social proof (testimonials, metrics, user count)

### 3. **Storytelling Flow**

**Issues:**
- Story jumps around (personal → product → values)
- No clear narrative arc
- "Why we exist" and "What we believe" cards are too brief

### 4. **Trust & Credibility**

**Missing:**
- No concrete numbers or metrics
- No testimonials
- No team photos or bios
- No company timeline/milestones
- No press/mentions

**New Content:**
```
The Problem We Solve

Founders waste months (and thousands of dollars) on ideas that won't work because they don't have access to:
- Market research and competitive analysis
- Financial modeling and projections

- Personalized recommendations based on their unique constraints

We automate the research and analysis that advisors do, so you can focus on building.
```

**Current:**

**Improved:**

```
What We Believe

1. Ideas must fit the founder
   The best startup idea for you is one that aligns with your goals, skills, time, and budget. Generic advice doesn't work.

2. Validation before building
```
