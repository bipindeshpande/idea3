# Customer Journey Map

## Overview
A Customer Journey Map visualizes the complete experience a customer has with your product or service, from first awareness through long-term engagement. This template helps you identify pain points, opportunities, and moments that matter.

## Why Map the Customer Journey
- **Understand customer experience** - See the journey from their perspective
- **Identify pain points** - Find where customers struggle
- **Discover opportunities** - Uncover moments to delight
- **Align team** - Shared understanding of customer experience
- **Improve conversion** - Optimize each stage of the journey

## How to Create Your Journey Map

### Step 1: Research
- Interview 10+ customers about their experience
- Review analytics data for each stage
- Analyze support tickets and feedback
- Survey customers at different journey stages

### Step 2: Map the Journey
- Document each stage in detail
- Identify all touchpoints
- Note customer thoughts and feelings
- List pain points and opportunities

### Step 3: Prioritize
- Focus on high-impact pain points first
- Identify quick wins
- Plan longer-term improvements
- Set metrics for each stage

### Step 4: Take Action
- Fix critical pain points
- Implement high-impact opportunities
- Test improvements
- Measure results

### Step 5: Iterate
- Update journey map quarterly
- Re-interview customers
- Track metrics over time
- Continuously improve

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Mapping the journey you want, not the real journey
- Not talking to actual customers
- Focusing only on your product, not the full experience
- Ignoring emotional aspects
- Not prioritizing pain points
- Creating the map and never using it

---

## Next Steps

1. **Fix critical pain points** - Address high-impact issues first

3. **Measure improvements** - Track metrics for each stage
4. **Update regularly** - Revisit journey map quarterly
5. **Share with team** - Ensure everyone understands the customer journey

---

*Remember: The customer journey is about their experience, not your product. Focus on making each stage better for them.*
