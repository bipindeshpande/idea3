# Feature Prioritization Framework

## Overview
This framework helps you decide which features to build first by evaluating them across multiple dimensions: customer value, business impact, effort, and risk. Use it to make data-driven decisions about your product roadmap.

## Why Prioritize Features
- **Focus on what matters** - Build features customers actually want
- **Maximize impact** - Get the most value from limited resources
- **Reduce waste** - Avoid building features nobody uses
- **Align team** - Shared understanding of priorities
- **Move faster** - Clear priorities enable faster decisions

---

## Feature Evaluation Criteria

### 1. Customer Value
*How much does this feature matter to customers?*

**Scoring (1-10):**
- 1-3: Nice to have, low impact
- 4-6: Important, moderate impact
- 7-8: Very important, high impact
- 9-10: Critical, must-have

**Questions to Ask:**
- How many customers requested this?
- How often do customers mention this need?
- Would customers pay more for this?
- Does this solve a critical pain point?

**Score:** [1-10]

---

### 2. Business Impact
*How does this feature impact business goals?*

**Scoring (1-10):**
- 1-3: Minimal business impact
- 4-6: Moderate impact on revenue/growth
- 7-8: Significant impact on key metrics
- 9-10: Critical for business success

**Impact Areas:**
- [ ] Revenue growth
- [ ] Customer acquisition
- [ ] Customer retention
- [ ] Competitive advantage
- [ ] Operational efficiency

**Score:** [1-10]

---

### 3. Effort Required
*How much work is needed to build this?*

**Scoring (1-10, where 10 = most effort):**
- 1-3: Quick win, low effort
- 4-6: Moderate effort, weeks of work
- 7-8: Significant effort, months of work
- 9-10: Major effort, requires major resources

**Effort Components:**
- Development time: [Days/Weeks/Months]
- Design work: [Low/Medium/High]
- Testing complexity: [Low/Medium/High]
- Dependencies: [None/Some/Many]

**Score:** [1-10]

---

### 4. Risk Level
*What are the risks of building this feature?*

**Scoring (1-10, where 10 = highest risk):**
- 1-3: Low risk, well-understood
- 4-6: Moderate risk, some unknowns
- 7-8: High risk, significant unknowns
- 9-10: Very high risk, major uncertainty

**Risk Factors:**
- [ ] Technical complexity
- [ ] Market uncertainty
- [ ] Customer demand uncertainty
- [ ] Resource constraints
- [ ] Dependencies on other work

**Score:** [1-10]

## Prioritization Methods

### Method 1: Value vs Effort Matrix
Plot features on a 2x2 matrix:
- X-axis: Effort (Low to High)
- Y-axis: Value (Low to High)
- Focus on: High Value, Low Effort quadrant

### Method 2: RICE Scoring
- **Reach:** How many users will this affect?
- **Impact:** How much will it impact each user? (0.25 to 3)
- **Confidence:** How confident are you? (50% to 100%)
- **Effort:** How much work? (person-months)

**RICE Score = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) / Effort**

### Method 3: Kano Model
Categorize features as:
- **Basic:** Must-haves, expected
- **Performance:** More is better
- **Delight:** Unexpected, wow factor

## Decision Framework

### Build Now
- High customer value (7+)
- High business impact (7+)
- Low to medium effort (1-6)
- Low to medium risk (1-6)
- Priority score > 1.0

### Plan for Next Quarter
- High customer value (7+)
- High business impact (7+)
- High effort (7-10)
- Medium risk (4-7)
- Priority score 0.7-1.0

### Defer
- Medium customer value (4-6)
- Medium business impact (4-6)
- Any effort level
- Priority score 0.4-0.7

### Don't Build
- Low customer value (1-3)
- Low business impact (1-3)
- High effort (7-10)
- Priority score < 0.4

- [ ] Scores based on data, not assumptions
- [ ] Customer value validated with users
- [ ] Business impact aligned with goals
- [ ] Effort estimates are realistic
- [ ] Risks are identified and assessed
- [ ] Priority scores calculated
- [ ] Roadmap reflects priorities

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Prioritizing based on "squeaky wheel" customers
- Building features because competitors have them
- Not validating customer value
- Underestimating effort
- Ignoring risks
- Not revisiting priorities regularly

---

## Next Steps

1. **Evaluate all features** using this framework
2. **Calculate priority scores** for each feature
3. **Create prioritized roadmap** based on scores
4. **Validate with customers** - Confirm high-priority features
5. **Review quarterly** - Update priorities as you learn

---

*Remember: Prioritization is about saying no to good ideas so you can say yes to great ones. Focus on features that deliver the most value with the least effort.*
