# Go-to-Market Strategy Template

## Overview
A Go-to-Market (GTM) strategy outlines how you'll bring your product to market, acquire customers, and achieve product-market fit. This template helps you create a comprehensive plan for launching and scaling your startup.

## Why You Need a GTM Strategy
- **Focus your efforts** - Clear plan for customer acquisition
- **Align team** - Everyone knows the strategy
- **Measure progress** - Track what's working
- **Reduce risk** - Test assumptions before scaling
- **Accelerate growth** - Systematic approach to scaling

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Targeting too broad a market
- Not validating value proposition
- Ignoring competition
- Pricing without research
- Trying too many channels at once
- Not measuring what matters
- Scaling before product-market fit

---

## Next Steps

1. **Validate assumptions** - Test each section with real customers
2. **Start small** - Focus on one channel first
3. **Measure everything** - Track key metrics from day one
4. **Iterate quickly** - Adjust based on data
5. **Scale what works** - Double down on successful channels

---

*Remember: A great GTM strategy is one you can execute. Start simple, test assumptions, and scale what works.*
