# User Persona Template

## Overview
A user persona is a detailed representation of your ideal customer based on real data and research. This template helps you create comprehensive personas that guide product decisions, marketing strategies, and feature development.

## Why User Personas Matter
- **Focus product development** on real user needs
- **Improve marketing messaging** by speaking directly to your audience
- **Guide feature prioritization** based on user goals
- **Align team understanding** of who you're building for

## How to Use This Template

### Step 1: Research
- Interview 5-10 potential customers
- Review customer support tickets
- Analyze user analytics data
- Survey existing users
- Study competitor reviews

### Step 2: Identify Patterns
- Group similar users together
- Find common goals and pain points
- Identify shared behaviors
- Note demographic similarities

### Step 3: Create Personas
- Focus on 2-3 primary personas
- Make them specific, not generic
- Use real data, not assumptions
- Include both demographics and psychographics

### Step 4: Validate
- Share personas with your team
- Test assumptions with real users
- Update personas as you learn more
- Revisit quarterly

---

## Persona Validation Checklist

- [ ] Based on real user interviews (not assumptions)
- [ ] Includes specific goals and pain points
- [ ] Reflects actual user behavior patterns
- [ ] Team agrees on persona accuracy
- [ ] Used to guide product decisions
- [ ] Updated regularly with new insights

---

## Tips for Effective Personas

1. **Be specific:** "Marketing manager at a 50-person SaaS company" is better than "business professional"
2. **Use real quotes:** Include actual quotes from interviews
3. **Focus on behavior:** What they do, not just who they are
4. **Keep it relevant:** Only include details that impact your product
5. **Make them memorable:** Use names and photos to bring personas to life
6. **Update regularly:** Personas should evolve as you learn more

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Creating too many personas (focus on 2-3 primary ones)
- Making personas too generic or stereotypical
- Basing personas on assumptions instead of data
- Creating personas and never using them
- Not updating personas as you learn more

---

## Next Steps

1. **Use personas in product planning:** Reference personas when prioritizing features
2. **Guide marketing messaging:** Write copy that speaks directly to persona pain points
3. **Inform design decisions:** Design for your primary persona's needs
4. **Test with real users:** Validate that your personas match reality
5. **Iterate:** Update personas quarterly based on new insights

---

*Remember: Personas are tools to help you build better products. They should guide decisions, not replace talking to real users.*
